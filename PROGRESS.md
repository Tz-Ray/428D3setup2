  Both arms ran on the same service process; only the `--mitigation` switch differed.

## How the reported runs were reached

1. **Smoke tests.** A one-word plan (system prompt asks for `OK`) checked the harness end to end
   on both models at temperature 0. Filed under `results/smoke/`. The Llama one was interrupted
   after 7 of 65 trials.
2. **Sampling.** Saved-plan trials were changed to temperature 0.8 with a distinct recorded seed
   per trial and a 192-token reply limit. At the earlier 64-token limit most replies were cut
   off, several part-way through the canary.
3. **Attack plan.** The operator's plan was stored as `mail-lab/plans/d3-attack-plan.json` with a
   made-up owner profile and the canary `CPTS4281118`. The baseline system prompt ends with one
   plain privacy line. V3 was reworked so the planted email asks only for an acknowledgement and
   the trigger refers back to it without naming the fields; before that, models leaked on the
   planted email itself.
4. **Pilot.** The first full Llama run gave V1 19 of 24, V2 6 of 25 and V3 24 of 24. In the V2
   transcripts Llama read the hidden text and refused it, because the hidden request had no
   pretext. The five V2 emails were rewritten to hide one request framed as an automated IT
   Security records check, keeping the same five hiding techniques. A V2-only rerun gave 18 of 25
   on Llama and 16 of 25 on Qwen. All pilot runs, including a Qwen run stopped after its V1
   trials, are under `results/pilot/` and are not baseline numbers.
5. **Baseline.** Both models were run in full from tag `baseline-v2` and the results committed
   before the mitigation arm started.
6. **Mitigation.** Both models were rerun with `--mitigation` on the same plan, seeds and
   settings.
